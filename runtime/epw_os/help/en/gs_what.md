# What EPW OS Is

EPW OS (Ela Power Watch) is an automation and visualization system for
electrical installations — its job is to **show the state of the
installation and let you control it from the screen**: reading digital
input/output states, reading analog point measurements, controlling
switching devices (breakers, contactors) from the one-line diagram,
recording events and alarms, and exporting historical data.

EPW OS **is not a protection system for the installation**. See
[Protection](help://saf_principle) — this is one of the most important
principles this whole system is built on.

The program runs in one of two modes, shown on the status bar:

- **SIMULATION MODE** — input/output values are generated in software,
  with no real hardware. Used for training and testing.
- **LIVE MODE** — data comes from real devices connected to the system.
