# EPW OS — Help

**Version {version}**

This help system describes how EPW OS actually works — step by step,
based on the program's real behavior.

## What you'll find here

- **Getting Started** — what EPW OS is and how the screen is laid out
- **Access Levels** — User, Operator, Engineer, and who can do what
- **Digital Inputs/Outputs** and **Analog Points**
- **Main View** — the one-line diagram and manual control
- **Alarms**, **Events and Audit**, **Historical Data**
- **Protection** — including the "the screen informs, the hardware
  protects" principle
- **Kiosk Mode** and **On-Screen Keyboard**
- **Settings**
- **Troubleshooting** — ready answers to common questions

## How to use this help window

Three tabs on the left:

- **Contents** — the full chapter list; click a chapter to expand it,
  and a topic to display its content
- **Index** — an alphabetical list of terms linking to topics
- **Search** — type a word or phrase to search every topic's content

The **Back** and **Forward** buttons at the top work like a browser's —
they let you return to a previously opened topic. The **Hide** button
collapses the left panel to leave more room for the content.

This help window is available from anywhere in the program with **F1**,
at every access level, including in Kiosk Mode.
