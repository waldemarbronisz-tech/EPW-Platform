# The Measurement Panel

Below the device status panel is a measurement panel showing: phase
voltages (VL1, VL2, VL3), frequency, phase currents (IL1, IL2, IL3),
active power, reactive power, apparent power, power factor, and energy.

Values appear once the incoming supply (Q1) is closed — with Q1 open,
the panel shows zero values. These are simulated demonstration values,
not measurements from a real energy meter — shown in **amber**, not the
green used for a real reading, and stored in Historian with quality
**SIMULATED** rather than GOOD (see
[What Historian Records](help://hist_what)) so they can never be
mistaken for real measurement history later.
