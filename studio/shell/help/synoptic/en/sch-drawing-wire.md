# 5.2 Drawing a Wire Step by Step

Step by step: pick a medium (keys `1`/`2`/`3`, or the toolbar toggle - [5.3](help://synoptic/sch-media)), click a starting point (ideally on a symbol's terminal), click further points to add bends, and Enter finishes the wire. Escape cancels the wire currently being drawn.

Any diagonal mouse movement is automatically turned into two right-angle segments (horizontal first, then vertical) - a diagonal segment is never created. Backspace while drawing undoes the last point added (one bend), not the whole wire.

On an already-finished wire: Alt+click on one of its segments inserts a new bend exactly at the nearest point on that segment; dragging an existing wire point automatically inserts whatever bends are needed on either side so the whole wire stays orthogonal.
